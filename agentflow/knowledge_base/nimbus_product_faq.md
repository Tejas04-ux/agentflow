# Nimbus Cloud Storage – Product FAQ (Sample knowledge base)

## Plans and pricing
Nimbus offers three plans. Free includes 5 GB of storage for one user. Pro costs $9 per month and includes 1 TB
of storage with version history for 30 days. Team costs $15 per user per month, includes 5 TB of shared storage,
admin controls and version history for 180 days. Annual billing gives two months free.

## File limits
The maximum size of a single uploaded file is 5 GB on Free, 50 GB on Pro and 250 GB on Team.

## Sharing and security
Shared links can be protected with a password and an expiry date. All files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and
TLS 1.3 in transit. Two-factor authentication is available on every plan and is mandatory for Team admins.

## Refunds and cancellation
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the billing period. Annual plans are
eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans are not refunded.

## Support
Free users get community forum support. Pro users get email support with a 24-hour response target. Team users get
priority chat support with a 4-hour response target on business days.
